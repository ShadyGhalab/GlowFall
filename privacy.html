Privacy Policy

Glowfall is a mobile puzzle game provided by Tendonlife. This policy explains how information is handled when you play, use optional features, visit this website, or contact us. Data handling can vary by app version, region, device settings, and the choices you make.

Game progress and preferences

Glowfall stores game progress, recovery information, accessibility preferences, and other settings on your device so you can resume play. Your device or operating system may include app data in backups depending on your settings. Game progress does not require you to grant advertising tracking permission.

Advertising and your choices

Glowfall uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage ad privacy choices and deliver ads. Depending on your region, consent, and SDK configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions, usage information, and diagnostics. This information can be used to deliver ads, measure performance, and operate and protect the advertising service. Non-personalized ads may still involve processing data.

Where available, use Settings > Privacy and ads > Privacy Choices to review or change your regional advertising consent. The Personalized Ads setting and iOS tracking permission are separate from regional consent. You can change app tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You may play without granting tracking permission; optional ads or rewarded continues may be unavailable. Remove Ads is an optional purchase, not a requirement to decline tracking.

Learn more about Google's handling of information in its privacy policy and partner sites explanation.

Analytics, attribution, and crash reports

Earlier versions of Glowfall use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and AppsFlyer for usage measurement, technical diagnostics, and app attribution. Depending on the version and service configuration, information can include app or installation identifiers, device information, gameplay and settings events, purchase and advertising events, and crash diagnostics. These services help understand app performance and improve the game. App-owned Firebase and AppsFlyer measurement is disabled in the privacy-hardened release when that release becomes available; disabling measurement does not automatically delete information collected by earlier versions. Google AdMob may still process information for advertising independently.

See Firebase privacy information and AppsFlyer's privacy policy. Your tracking permission does not by itself represent consent to all analytics or diagnostic processing.

Game Center and purchases

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple processes Game Center account, leaderboard, score, and achievement information under its own terms. Purchases and restoration are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit; Glowfall uses verified purchase information to unlock purchased features. We do not ask for your payment card details in the game. See Apple's privacy policy.

Support and website

If you contact us, we receive the contact information and message you provide, including any diagnostic details you choose to share. We use it to respond to your request. Website hosting and network services may process connection information needed to provide and protect the site.

Purposes, service providers, and legal bases

Information is used to provide requested features, maintain and protect the game and website, handle purchases, deliver permitted advertising, resolve technical issues, and respond to support requests. Where data protection laws require consent, we rely on your relevant consent for the processing covered by that consent. Necessary service and purchase processing may rely on performance of a contract, and limited security, support, or legally required processing may rely on applicable legitimate interests or legal obligations. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own; their privacy notices describe their practices and applicable transfer safeguards.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on the device until removed or reset, subject to the operating system's backup and restoration behavior. Resetting game progress does not necessarily remove every preference, data held by Apple or advertising providers, or prior analytics information. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data from the device, subject to backups and restoration.

Information held by us is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, including resolving support requests and meeting applicable legal obligations. Information processed by service providers is subject to their retention settings and applicable policies. You can contact us to request information about retention and deletion of personal data associated with your use of Glowfall. We may need enough information to locate the relevant records and verify your request; please do not send payment credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the advertising controls described above to change ad choices, and contact us for other privacy requests. Information held independently by Apple, Google, or other providers may require a request through the relevant provider's privacy controls.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests concerning personal data, or support, contact support@glowfall.app.
